# Jandí — credits

## Palette

The eight structural tones (Suco verde, Brisa, Oby, Jandí, Genipina,
Nhandí, Yandí, Tinta de guerra) are used unchanged from
[jandi-colors](https://github.com/franciscotbjr/jandi-colors) by
franciscotbjr, MIT licence. That project credits its own sources: the
genipin research of UNICAMP/Lasefi (Maria Isabel Neves, Monique Strieder,
Maria Angela Meireles), Silveira Bueno's *Vocabulário Tupi-Guarani*, and
the Instituto Socioambiental's *Povos Indígenas no Brasil*.

The terminal hues (urucum, the jenipapo leaf and ripe fruit, açaí) are this
theme's own additions, chosen from the same world as the pigment and
checked for at least 4:1 WCAG contrast against the background.

## Backgrounds

`1-polpa` (the fresh Suco verde pulp, with Brisa blue creeping in from one corner as the air begins to oxidize it) and `2-oxidacao` (the full eight-tone ramp, juice to ink) are generated from the palette itself. No third-party material, and
deliberately no indigenous body-painting patterns (grafismos): those designs
belong to specific peoples. Same licence as the theme.
